# Odran Morrow — Necromancer

*The Keeper of Names*

An undead archivist who remembers the names that the living would rather forget.

## Who they are

Odran was the keeper of a funerary archive, recording travelers who died far from home. When the archive collapsed into the dungeon, he refused to leave its unburied dead and returned years later as something quieter than a living man. He now descends with a ledger of unfinished names, borrowing the strength of old bones to recover the records and return each name to someone who remembers it.

Recover the lost archive and release the dead from being remembered only as monsters.

Personality: Courteous and unsettlingly patient; Gentle with the grieving; Possesses a very dry sense of humor.

Addresses skeletal companions by name; dusts a page before reading it; pauses as if listening to a distant reply.

Voice: Measured and soft, with the deliberate diction of someone accustomed to preserving exact words.

> “A name is a small thing. Until it is the last thing.”

## Specialty and powers

Raising persistent bone thralls and combining their direct damage with spellcraft and life-stealing equipment.

### Raise Dead

Start the run with one bone thrall. Defeated monsters can add another, up to three total. Each thrall contributes 2 direct damage per turn before enemy shield absorption. Thralls persist between floors.

Art direction: Restrained skeletal retainers in a separately requested battle illustration; the default character sprite shows Odran alone so his silhouette remains clear.

### Fang siphon

Vampire Fang supplies 5 direct damage and up to 2 healing from actual direct damage dealt, including a killing blow. Overkill does not grant additional healing.

Art direction: A small pale fang talisman or ritual blade. Any siphon effect is brief and secondary, not a permanent red aura.

The skull staff is visual equipment. Bone thralls deal ordinary direct damage subject to enemy shield absorption, not automatic piercing damage.

## Physical appearance for character art

### Body, silhouette and bearing

Undead human; an actual skeletal figure, not a living person wearing a skull mask. Formerly an older adult, approximately 62 at death; years since death unspecified. Masculine presentation; approximately 181 cm in-world. 

Tall and spare, with a convincing human skeletal frame beneath layered funerary cloth. Narrow bony shoulders gain volume from the mantle; avoid a tiny floating skull on a shapeless robe.

Deep-plum cowl around a pale skull, a broad carved-bone mantle, moss-green robe and a tall skull-topped staff on the viewer's right.

Patient, dignified and quietly uncanny. Convey his attention through skull angle and hands rather than a cartoon grin.

### Head and face

Exposed anatomical skull inside the cowl, with dark sockets and tiny muted jade lights; no living skin or anime human face.

Elongated but proportionate skull with clear cheekbones, a broad lower jaw, simplified tooth rhythm and a small dark nasal cavity.

Old ivory bone with quiet cool gray-green shadows, a small repaired chip along the cheek ridge and restrained wear. Keep the skull readable without drawing every tooth as a bright stripe.

Dark recessed sockets with small low-intensity pale-jade accents; the skull staff must not be mistaken for a second character head.

None. Do not add eyebrows, beard, hair or flesh to the standard skeletal identity.

Heavy plum cowl with a broad dark opening and layered folds surrounding the skull without merging into its jaw.

### Clothing and hands

Carved-bone shoulder mantle over muted moss cloth, dark inner tunic, plum sleeves and an aged green stone clasp. Bone pieces are ceremonial shapes, not a noisy pile of miniature skulls.

Layered moss-green funerary robe with separated panels and a ragged but controlled hem, a narrow dark belt with a small bone buckle, and dark wrapped feet or simple burial shoes visible beneath the cloth.

Deep-plum outer shroud hangs behind the moss robe with long asymmetric folds and readable separation from the legs.

Skeletal hands with a clear thumb, palm mass and simplified finger groups. Staff-side fingers close around the shaft; the other hand rests slightly open in a calm guiding gesture.

### Weapons and equipment

**Reliquary skull staff** — A worn dark wooden shaft capped with a small bone skull and an aged iron brow band. A short plum binding hangs below the skull. Keep the shaft inside a skeletal hand grip and the skull head well away from the portrait.

His signature casting focus, carried with quiet ceremonial authority.

**Vampire Fang** — An ivory fang in a dark metal fitting, small enough to remain secondary to the staff and bone mantle.

An optional visible belt talisman in the default staff pose.

**Ember Grimoire** — A dark practical spellbook with restrained binding, not a floating oversized prop beside the head.

A spellbook that can remain stowed.

### Recognizable identity features

- Actual ivory skull framed by a plum cowl
- Carved-bone mantle over moss-green robes
- Tiny muted jade socket accents
- A small staff reliquary skull, kept separate from his head

### Pose and effects

Upright, relaxed and alert, slight three-quarter turn toward the viewer's right, grounded feet and a natural weight shift.

At most a faint muted jade breath-like wisp during casting. No bright permanent green flames, huge skull halo or companions in the default solo portrait/sprite.

### Portrait framing

Center Odran's own cowl-framed skull and preserve the full jaw, hood crown and a little bone mantle. Remove the staff skull, shaft, fang, floating spirits and other heads; reconstruct any obscured cowl edge.

### Avoid

- A living face painted onto the skull
- A floating skull detached from the neck
- Rows of bright individually outlined teeth
- The staff skull inside the head portrait
- Huge neon-green aura
- Unconvincing skeletal anatomy
