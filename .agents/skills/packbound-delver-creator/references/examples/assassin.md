# Sable Rook — Assassin

*The Dusk Runner*

A former smuggler and message-runner who treats every locked door as a conversation she has not finished.

## Who they are

Sable grew up carrying sealed messages through a district where official couriers refused to go. She learned to steal keys before she learned to trust employers. When a guild began using her routes to traffic people instead of goods, she stole its ledger and disappeared. Dungeon work funds the safe houses she now maintains under names nobody can trace back to her.

Buy freedom for the people named in the stolen ledger and find the guild's hidden route through the dungeon.

Personality: Wry and guarded; Meticulous about promises; Protective of people who cannot fight back.

Turns a coin across her knuckles while listening; watches hands rather than faces; always notes a second exit.

Voice: Low and direct, with a dry edge and very little wasted explanation.

> “You did not see me. That was the kind version.”

## Specialty and powers

Eluding enemy attacks with timed invisibility, then exploiting venom and shield-piercing dagger strikes.

### Shadowstep

Invisible on turns 1, 4, 7 and every third turn thereafter. Monsters cannot target her, so their strikes and on-hit effects are skipped. Existing poison, reflection and item costs still apply.

Art direction: A brief reduction to a coherent shadow silhouette during activation. The idle character remains visible and readable; no automatic clone or large smoke cloud.

### Venom edge

Venom Dagger provides 5 direct damage, 3 poison and 2 shield piercing. These effects come from her dagger rather than an innate power.

Art direction: A restrained greenish material tint near the blade edge only when depicting poison; no dripping fluorescent slime.

Her second dagger is a parrying tool rather than a source of extra attacks.

## Physical appearance for character art

### Body, silhouette and bearing

Human. Adult, approximately 31. Woman presentation; approximately 173 cm in-world. 

Wiry and athletic, with compact shoulders, balanced hips and strong flexible legs. The costume fits a practical adult runner; avoid exaggerated curves or a stocky toy-like silhouette.

A deep-plum pointed hood above narrow petrol-teal leather armor, a split dark cloak and two compact daggers held clear of the body.

Watchful and restrained, with a subtle head tilt and natural asymmetric elbows; relaxed readiness instead of a crouched attack pose.

### Head and face

Partial cloth mask covering nose, mouth and lower jaw; visible anime-inspired eyes and brow. Concealment is intentional.

Narrow oval head within a close-fitting hood; the upper cheek and brow communicate adult structure without enlarging the eyes.

Medium warm olive skin visible around the brow and upper cheeks only. The lower face remains hidden; never draw a mouth on top of the mask.

Amber-brown eyes with controlled dark upper lids, low expressive brows and a slightly narrowed, observant gaze.

Near-black hair, mostly concealed; a short side fringe can break the hood opening without covering both eyes.

Deep-plum fabric hood with a soft asymmetric peak and a dark inner opening; a muted teal scarf-mask wraps securely around the lower face.

### Clothing and hands

Fitted petrol-teal leather tunic with overlapping chest panels, small asymmetrical shoulder protection, dark forearm wraps, a narrow diagonal strap and minimal brass fasteners.

Charcoal fitted trousers with restrained teal knee reinforcement, dark plum-brown soft boots and a low-profile utility belt with one small pouch.

Split plum-black cloak falling behind the arms and legs, long enough for a distinctive silhouette but short enough to run without dragging.

Bare fingertips or fingerless gloves; clearly drawn palms, curled fingers and aligned wrists around each dagger grip.

### Weapons and equipment

**Venom Dagger** — Slim stepped steel blade, small antique-brass guard, dark wrapped grip and restrained pommel.

Her primary visual weapon.

**Off-hand parrying dagger** — A plain companion dagger of similar construction. One dagger may be held upright and the other in a relaxed reverse grip; each hand must visibly close around its own handle.

A defensive companion to her primary dagger, used to catch or deflect an incoming blade.

### Recognizable identity features

- Plum hood and teal lower-face mask
- A narrow amber-eyed gaze rather than an exposed full face
- Two deliberately held compact daggers
- Light asymmetrical armor with a runner's silhouette

### Pose and effects

Upright, relaxed and alert, slight three-quarter turn toward the viewer's right, grounded feet and a natural weight shift.

None in the default idle. Shadowstep effects belong on separate activation layers and must not hide the portrait.

### Portrait framing

Center the hood-framed upper face and mask, preserving the hood peak and masked chin shape. Remove dagger tips, straps that intrude on the face and any shadow-effect fragments. Keep the eyes readable without inventing an exposed mouth.

### Avoid

- A visible mouth through the mask
- Huge dagger blades
- Two ungripped floating weapons
- A seductive or exaggerated body pose
- Permanent smoke obscuring the face
- Unbalanced or toy-like proportions
