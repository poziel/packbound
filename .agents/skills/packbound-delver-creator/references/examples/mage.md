# Mira Vey — Mage

*The Twin Ember*

A young spellwright who believes fire and frost are two ways of asking the same question.

## Who they are

Mira trained in a modest workshop that repaired enchanted lamps, cold-storage seals and unreliable household charms. During an experiment to make one focus handle both heat and cold, she discovered that the workshop's oldest crystal answered to a pulse deep beneath the city. She follows that pulse into the dungeon, recording what she learns rather than trusting the academy's old warnings.

Understand the buried mana source and build magic that ordinary people can use safely.

Personality: Curious and precise; Warmly candid with friends; Impatient with needless ceremony.

Tests an idea with one small flame and one tiny ice crystal; speaks her measurements under her breath when concentrating.

Voice: Clear and thoughtful, becoming quick and animated when a discovery surprises her.

> “A little heat. A little patience. Watch.”

## Specialty and powers

Careful mana management and elemental spellcraft, visually expressed through simultaneous frost and fire.

### Spellweaving

Mira draws on a deeper mana reserve to weave elemental spells. Her specialty is choosing when to spend that reserve on offense or protection, balancing fire and frost as the fight unfolds.

Art direction: Two open palms supporting distinct elemental spells, with a restrained violet mana aura.

### Ember spell

Ember Grimoire costs 3 mana to deal 10 direct damage and apply 2 burn. This is an equipped item's effect, not an additional innate class ability.

Art direction: A compact orange-red flame with a pale gold heart above the viewer-right palm.

### Frost spell

Frost Wand costs 2 mana to deal 8 direct damage and reduce each enemy strike by 2 that turn. It is an optional focus she may acquire during her adventures.

Art direction: A faceted icy core and small curl of frost above the viewer-left palm. The idle illustration can show this established elemental identity independently of the current equipped loadout.

Her subtle aura expresses her connection to mana rather than a separate protective power.

## Physical appearance for character art

### Body, silhouette and bearing

Human. Young adult, approximately 25. Woman presentation; approximately 170 cm in-world. 

Slender adult proportions with natural shoulders, waist, hips and limbs. Elegance comes from posture and cloth volume, not an oversized head, exaggerated curves or stretched legs.

Uncovered silver-haired head above a fitted indigo bodice, plum shoulder mantle and layered split robes; both forearms extend gently forward and outward to show the two spells.

Focused and self-assured, relaxed enough to suggest she controls both spells without strain.

### Head and face

Clear exposed anime-inspired human face; no elf ears and no tall pointed hat.

Softly angular oval face, gently defined cheekbones, a small proportionate chin and a visible neck above the high collar.

Fair skin with warm peach undertones, clean simplified facial planes, small straight nose and a restrained closed mouth. Avoid cold gray skin caused by overusing the hair palette.

Blue-violet irises, deliberate dark upper lids, coherent three-quarter gaze and expressive but proportionate anime eye shapes.

Silver, chin-to-shoulder-length layered hair with faint lavender shadows; a side-swept fringe frames the forehead, one side tucked back. Broad connected locks and a few purposeful light clusters preserve volume.

No head covering in the default design. Any hood is folded behind the neck and must not obscure the face.

### Clothing and hands

Fitted high-neck indigo bodice, shaped shoulders, plum short mantle, restrained antique-silver clasps and narrow pale trim. A small violet mana gem sits at the collar.

Overlapping indigo and plum robe panels form an open asymmetric overskirt. Dark fitted leggings and practical dark calf-height boots remain visible beneath it; cloth folds are broad and readable.

A short shoulder mantle above flowing split robe panels, avoiding a solid cone-shaped robe that hides all leg structure.

Both bare hands are visible with clear wrists, thumbs and softly curved fingers. Palms turn upward at waist-to-lower-chest height; forearms are gently forward, not rigidly extended sideways.

### Weapons and equipment

**Fire and frost manifestations** — Both hands open, palms facing upward and gently forward. Each compact spell hovers just above its palm with a small air gap; fingers, wrists and face remain visible.

Her signature expression of elemental control; the manifestations remain compact and distinct.

**Ember Grimoire** — A small dark-bound book with a restrained ember seal, optionally attached at the belt. It must not compete with the hands or enter the head portrait.

Stowed rather than held in the default two-palm design.

**Frost Wand** — When specifically requested in a variant, use a slender dark shaft with a clear ice-colored focus; preserve Mira's two-element identity.

Optional acquired item; not held in the default idle illustration.

### Recognizable identity features

- Silver hair with lavender shadow planes
- Visible blue-violet anime-inspired eyes
- Frost on the viewer's left and fire on the viewer's right
- Small violet collar gem and subtle mana wisps

### Pose and effects

Upright, relaxed and alert, slight three-quarter turn toward the viewer's right, grounded feet and a natural weight shift.

Compact frost crystal/curl above the viewer-left palm and orange-red flame above the viewer-right palm. Two or three subdued blue-violet wisps near the outer lower torso; no enclosing bubble, huge halo or particle shower. Local light may touch the nearest hands and cuffs.

### Portrait framing

Center the silver-haired head with complete locks and chin; retain the indigo collar, plum mantle and small collar gem when space permits. Exclude hands, fire, ice, aura fragments and book/wand edges. Keep warm facial colors and readable eyes.

### Avoid

- Childlike/chibi proportions
- Pointed elf ears
- Tall wizard hat hiding the hair
- Spells obscuring her face or fingers
- A staff occupying either open palm
- A large aura ring or constant glitter
