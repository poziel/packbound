# Monster concept prompt

Fill the brief below and supply the relevant inspected reference images. Use the
approved Packbound characters for rendering, not their human proportions or their
old source resolution. Choose the actual body/canvas dimensions before adapting
the concept into native artwork.

```text
Create one original Packbound monster: {species/body plan}, {enemy role},
{build and distinctive anatomy}, with {one or two identifying features}.

Show its complete body, including every ear, horn, wing, tail and foot, in
{species-appropriate pose and facing}. Make its weight and limb attachments
convincing. Head treatment: {eyes/muzzle/face/helmet}. Equipment, if any:
{equipment and readable grip/attachment}. Materials: {palette and materials}.
Effects: {none, or explicitly specified restrained effects}.

Match the supplied Packbound reference rendering: a clear silhouette, broad
material planes, readable thickness, deliberate connected pixel clusters,
stepped contours and selective highlights. Use coherent structural detail and
controlled value separation. Group fur/scales/feathers into readable masses.
Avoid decorative speckles, muddy fine texture, thick uniform outlines, generic
polygon anatomy and unrequested glow. Preserve this creature's distinctive
body plan and identity.

Composition: a single isolated creature on a truly transparent background,
with clean margins and the entire silhouette visible. No scenery, frame, text,
ground shadow, duplicate views or portrait inset. Light and effects must not
obscure its face or anatomy.

Production target: {chosen body width x height in source pixels; neither may
exceed 256 px}, {small/medium/large creature and intended relative size},
{chosen canvas dimensions}, {baseline/anchor}, {intended battle display size}.
These describe the intended native adaptation; the generated concept will be
checked and refined on that actual source grid. Preserve natural proportions
instead of stretching the body to satisfy the canvas. 256 px is a maximum,
not a fill target: rats, slimes and other small creatures use smaller bodies.
Include anatomical appendages in the body bounds; exclude padding, held
equipment and detached effects. Keep small creatures visibly smaller in battle
instead of automatically fitting every sprite to the same display dimensions.

Reference roles: {each supplied image and the rendering/anatomy feature it
informs}. Create an original monster rather than copying a reference identity.
```

Keep the exact filled prompt in the asset's version directory. Judge the result
inside the battle framing as well as enlarged; resolution alone does not establish
the quality of the design.
