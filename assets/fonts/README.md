# Interface fonts

The active interface uses **Pixel Operator Regular**, Jayvee Enaguas's fine
16-pixel raster typeface. `ui_pixel.tres` embeds an unmodified copy of
`PixelOperator.ttf` with antialiasing, subpixel positioning and hinting disabled.
The regular face keeps its thin strokes in headings, controls and explanations.

Downloaded on 2026-10-15 from the author's listing:
https://www.dafont.com/pixel-operator.font

The author's 2018-10-04 distribution is released under **CC0 1.0**. The complete
notice is included as `PixelOperator-CC0.txt`. The original TTF is preserved;
no system installation or network connection is needed at runtime.

Earlier Barlow Medium, Bitter, Jacquard 12 and Silkscreen fonts, their licenses,
and their rendering resources remain available for previous design iterations.
Their sources are the corresponding `ofl/` folders in https://github.com/google/fonts.
The Packbound title logo is separate artwork, not a rendered font resource.
