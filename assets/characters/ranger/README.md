# Rowan Thorne — Ranger

*The Wayfinder*

A wilderness courier who knows forgotten roads better than the people who drew the maps.

## Who they are

Rowan carried medicine and letters between isolated hill villages long before he became an archer for hire. After a familiar route vanished beneath a spreading sinkhole, he began tracing the old passages below it. He enters the dungeon to discover where those roads went, and to recover the people and supplies lost with them.

Reopen a safe route between the scattered villages and bring back evidence of what is swallowing the old roads.

Personality: Observant and lightly mischievous; Patient with frightened animals; Distrustful of grand promises but loyal to practical kindness.

Pauses to listen before opening a door, knots spare cord around an arrow shaft and remembers people by the routes they travel.

Voice: Easy, economical speech with a quiet joke at an unexpectedly tense moment.

> “There is a way through. There usually is.”

## Specialty and powers

Accurate ranged attacks, rapid volleys and arranging bows beside quivers to make every shot count.

### Volley

Every third turn, all equipped ranged items activate twice. Quiver adjacency strengthens applicable ranged activations.

Art direction: Quick, measured bow shots with a clear draw-and-release motion. Use ordinary arrows by default; magical trails are not an inherent class power.

shortbow is displayed as Ashwood Bow; quiver is displayed as Hunting Quiver.

## Physical appearance for character art

### Body, silhouette and bearing

Human. Adult, approximately 29. Man presentation; approximately 179 cm in-world. 

Lean and athletic, with flexible shoulders, a compact torso and strong but unexaggerated legs. His posture suggests stamina and easy movement rather than heavy lifting.

Forest-green hood framing a visible angled face, ochre scarf, fitted leather shoulder armor, a long split cloak and tall wooden bow on the viewer's right.

Calm, watchful and approachable, with a slight suggestion of a smile rather than a broad grin.

### Head and face

Visible anime-inspired adult face within an open hood.

Slightly angular oval face with a defined cheekbone, narrow jaw and proportionate chin.

Warm light-to-medium skin, a straight simplified nose, small relaxed mouth and softly arched brows. Keep the three-quarter perspective coherent and give the far eye less visible width.

Muted green eyes with deliberate dark upper lids and a focused, alert gaze toward the viewer's right.

Dark chestnut-brown hair swept diagonally across the forehead, with short pointed locks emerging from the hood. Keep hair as structured masses rather than many thin strands.

An open forest-green hood with a soft pointed crown and broad shaded folds; it frames the face without casting it into unreadable darkness.

### Clothing and hands

Green layered tunic beneath fitted brown leather shoulder and forearm armor; a warm ochre scarf around the neck; diagonal leather quiver strap and a small antique-brass clasp.

Charcoal trousers, overlapping green tunic tails, brown belt and a small practical pouch. Wrapped leather knee and shin protection above sturdy walking boots.

Long muted-green cloak hanging behind the viewer-left side, split enough to reveal both legs and grounded feet.

Fingerless or open-finger leather bracers with visible warm skin. The bow hand encloses the riser; the resting hand hangs naturally with relaxed fingers.

### Weapons and equipment

**Ashwood recurve bow** — Warm brown wooden limbs, a darker wrapped riser and one continuous taut string. At rest, the viewer-right hand wraps the riser with the thumb and fingers visibly overlapping it; do not draw the string across his face.

Corresponds to the Ashwood Bow inventory item; the existing accepted design uses a tall recurved silhouette.

**Hunting quiver** — Worn brown leather shoulder quiver with a restrained group of cream-feathered arrows. It sits behind the viewer-left shoulder and remains separate from the head in a portrait.

Starting inventory item and signature visual equipment.

**Trail knife** — A small plain utility knife on the belt, normally sheathed and visually secondary to the bow.

Optional sheathed costume tool; adds no inventory ability.

### Recognizable identity features

- Chestnut forelock inside an open green hood
- Ochre scarf against muted green clothing
- Quiver behind the viewer-left shoulder
- Tall recurved bow with a readable wrapped grip

### Pose and effects

Upright, relaxed and alert, slight three-quarter turn toward the viewer's right, grounded feet and a natural weight shift.

None in the default idle design.

### Portrait framing

Optically center the hood-framed face, keep hair, hood crown and chin complete, and retain a little ochre scarf. Remove arrow fletching, quiver edges, bow/string and unrelated cloak fragments; reconstruct obscured hood or hair.

### Avoid

- A bowstring crossing the face
- Quiver arrows filling the portrait margins
- Bulbous mouth or nose clusters
- A warrior's heavy plate silhouette
- Mandatory orange/cyan rim lighting
