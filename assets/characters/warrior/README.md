# Garran Vale — Warrior

*The Last Watch*

A former bridge-watch captain who keeps counting the people behind him, even when he travels alone.

## Who they are

Garran spent most of his adult life guarding a river crossing used by merchants and displaced families. When the watch was ordered to close the gates during an evacuation, he stayed outside with the final wagon. He survived; his command did not. He now takes dungeon contracts to buy safe passage and supplies for settlements that cannot pay for protection.

Find the missing members of his watch and earn a future in which nobody is left outside a locked gate.

Personality: Patient and dryly funny; Protective without being patronizing; Careful with equipment and generous with food.

Checks shield straps before answering a difficult question; quietly moves himself between danger and the least experienced person in the room.

Voice: Short, practical sentences with a calm, reassuring delivery.

> “Behind me. We can argue when everyone is home.”

## Specialty and powers

Holding the line, protecting companions and turning a completely defended enemy action into a decisive counterattack.

### Riposte

When an enemy action is completely defended and the enemy remains alive, counterattack for 4 plus current plating damage. This retaliation bypasses enemy shield protection.

Art direction: A compact, precise sword counter following a planted shield guard. Communicate discipline and weight; no permanent magical aura.

The legacy longsword item ID is currently displayed as Iron Dagger. Garran's illustrated arming sword is costume equipment, not a promise about his starting item.

## Physical appearance for character art

### Body, silhouette and bearing

Human. Adult, approximately 38. Man presentation; approximately 185 cm in-world. 

Broad-shouldered, muscular and work-hardened, with a substantial chest, compact waist and strong legs. Armor follows believable joints rather than inflating the shoulders or boots.

A tall, grounded steel knight: narrow closed helmet, swept scarlet crest, substantial articulated shoulders, tapering shield on the viewer's right and lowered sword on the viewer's left.

The face remains concealed; confidence comes from the helmet angle, relaxed shoulders and controlled equipment placement.

### Head and face

Fully closed steel helmet. The visor is intentionally dark; do not add a visible human face or two glowing eyes.

Angular enclosed helm with a pronounced central brow ridge, narrow dark eye slit and long vertical faceplate opening. The helmet remains integrated with the neck and shoulders.

Concealed in the standard design. For a separately requested unhelmeted variant only: square jaw, warm tan skin, hazel eyes and a short scar beneath the left side of the chin.

Concealed. Unhelmeted identity only: short dark-brown hair graying at the temples; never show it through the visor.

Scarlet crest sweeping backward from the helmet crown; use broad connected red clusters and preserve its complete silhouette in portraits.

### Clothing and hands

Cool steel breastplate with clear planar highlights, layered rounded pauldrons, articulated forearm plates and dark mail visible at the joints. Brown leather harness and a small brass cloak clasp.

Layered steel hip plates over dark mail, fitted brown belt with brass buckle, red hanging cloth between the legs, articulated knee guards and practical steel sabatons.

Deep scarlet cloak attached at the shoulders, falling behind the body with a broad asymmetric drape and a lightly weathered hem.

Fully enclosed steel gauntlets. Sword fingers visibly close around the hilt; shield arm supports the shield's weight through a coherent forearm position.

### Weapons and equipment

**Watchman's arming sword** — A straight double-edged steel blade, simple antique-brass crossguard, dark leather grip and small pommel. Grip sits between guard and pommel.

Signature visual equipment; not a separate implemented inventory item.

**Scarlet kite shield** — A tall tapering red shield with steel edging and a simple pale four-point emblem; worn finish in broad material clusters, supported by a hand grip and forearm straps.

Signature visual equipment; actual protection is determined by equipped inventory items.

### Recognizable identity features

- Closed angular steel helm with backward red crest
- Scarlet shoulder cloak and matching shield
- Pale four-point shield emblem
- A planted, reassuring stance rather than an aggressive lunge

### Pose and effects

Upright, relaxed and alert, slight three-quarter turn toward the viewer's right, grounded feet and a natural weight shift.

None in the default idle design.

### Portrait framing

Center the helmet and crest as the identity, preserve the entire crest and lower faceplate, and include a small amount of scarlet collar and steel shoulder. Exclude sword and shield; repair any overlaps from the concept.

### Avoid

- Exposed face through the visor
- Huge pauldrons or swollen boots
- A hand merely touching the sword edge
- A permanent fire or cyan aura
- Dense scratches or heraldic decoration
