# Dusksteel Blade

Epic weapon. Stable ID: `dusksteel_blade`.

Its maker hammered through the last watch of night.

## Purpose and rules

Starting stats: 15 damage, 3 pierce. Each affordable normal melee activation costs 2 stamina; damage targets the current enemy. Piercing bypasses that amount of enemy block. Ordinary attacks activate once per round; explicit extra-activation effects may repeat them.

Duplicate inventory copies add independent contributions. Relics are unique per run. Fight-start block resets with the next fight; round resources refresh each round. Healing is capped at maximum health. No additional duration, cooldown, random proc, or hidden stacking bonus is supplied. No direct empowerment of neighboring items is granted. Normal existing booster effects may still affect eligible attacks and shields.

Trades two backpack cells and stamina for a stronger repeatable physical attack. Stamina sources support its activation; damage boosters improve eligible attacks.
