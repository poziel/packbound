# Moonwell Pebble

Rare focus; stable ID `moonwell_pebble`. Available to every class.

## Purpose and operation

Adds 3 maximum mana, refreshed each round.

Activates in the shared priority and backpack reading order, at most once per ordinary round before explicit repeat effects. All listed activation costs are checked together. No item-specific cooldown, random chance or once-per-fight cap is added. Unaffordable items skip without spending resources. Permanent stats apply only while equipped; combat state resets according to the shared combat rules. Companions are auto-activating equipment, not separately targetable units.

## Strengths and combinations

Passive support leaves activation resources for other equipment. Orthogonal adjacency support and available mana/stamina determine the best arrangement. Strategic combinations grant no unlisted bonus.

Supplies no adjacency or quiver bonus.

## Acquisition and crafting

Eligible for the rare item reward pool subject to class restrictions. No bespoke shop price or drop probability is assigned. Craft: Raincatch Bowl + Salt Lantern + Spark Ring → Moonwell Pebble. Outgoing recipes: The Unwritten Sun, Captive Aurora, Solar Crucible. Each recipe consumes all listed quantities and produces one result when combat starts, requiring one unlocked orthogonally connected packed group. No catalyst or gold cost. Incomplete or disconnected groups consume nothing; an output with no fitting space enters the stash. Larger complete recipes take priority; outputs do not chain at the same combat start.

## Lore

It is always found on the side of a well facing the moon.
