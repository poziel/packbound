# Venom Codex

A venom spell that deals 3 magical damage and applies 2 poison for 2 mana and no stamina. It activates each round when both resource costs can be paid, in priority and backpack reading order. Echo repeats its damage, condition and elemental trace contribution. Magical resistance reduces its damage; block absorbs direct damage after resistance. Conditions bypass block.

Poison ticks for its stack total and loses one stack each round. Conditions reset between encounters and can be cleansed. Elementalist casts leave a venom trace for two rounds. Other Delvers can use the spell without causing reactions.

The 2 × 2 footprint rotates during preparation. Damage boosters empower it through ordinary adjacency; it supplies no adjacency bonuses. It competes with other spells for mana. Elementalist and Necromancer offense rewards can offer it. There are no crafting recipes or merges.
