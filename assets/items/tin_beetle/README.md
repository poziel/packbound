# Tin Beetle

Common companion; stable ID `tin_beetle`. Available to every class.

## Purpose and operation

Deals 2 physical damage per affordable activation.

Activates in the shared priority and backpack reading order, at most once per ordinary round before explicit repeat effects. All listed activation costs are checked together. No item-specific cooldown, random chance or once-per-fight cap is added. Unaffordable items skip without spending resources. Permanent stats apply only while equipped; combat state resets according to the shared combat rules. Companions are auto-activating equipment, not separately targetable units.

## Strengths and combinations

Compact attacks leave room for support equipment; their resource cost competes with other attacks. Orthogonal adjacency support and available mana/stamina determine the best arrangement. Strategic combinations grant no unlisted bonus.

Supplies no adjacency or quiver bonus.

## Acquisition and crafting

Eligible for the common item reward pool subject to class restrictions. No bespoke shop price or drop probability is assigned. No recipe produces this item. Outgoing recipes: Pilgrim Crab, Gilded Scarab, Copper Cicada, Lantern Moth, Marrow Sparrow. Each recipe consumes all listed quantities and produces one result when combat starts, requiring one unlocked orthogonally connected packed group. No catalyst or gold cost. Incomplete or disconnected groups consume nothing; an output with no fitting space enters the stash. Larger complete recipes take priority; outputs do not chain at the same combat start.

## Lore

A clockmaker built it to keep lonely apprentices company.

## Visual design

A rounded tin beetle with six thick dark legs. Use a clear connected silhouette, broad material shading and restrained highlights. No incidental colored rim lights. The footprint is `#` at 32 source pixels per cell; empty cells remain transparent. All rotations are allowed. Layered editable art and exports are linked below; visual acceptance remains unreviewed.
