# Waxed Fletching

Uncommon booster; stable ID `waxed_fletching`. Restricted to ranger.

## Purpose and operation

While orthogonally touching the permanent Bowmaster quiver, adds 2 damage to Volley; this equipment remains in the backpack and is not consumed.

Activates in the shared priority and backpack reading order, at most once per ordinary round before explicit repeat effects. All listed activation costs are checked together. No item-specific cooldown, random chance or once-per-fight cap is added. Unaffordable items skip without spending resources. Permanent stats apply only while equipped; combat state resets according to the shared combat rules. Companions are auto-activating equipment, not separately targetable units.

## Strengths and combinations

Strengthens Volley rather than normal bow attacks, but occupies valuable cells touching the permanent quiver. Pairs with the permanent Bowmaster quiver and other different quiver attachments; normal bows receive no implicit bonus.

Touching bonuses are listed above; there are no other supplied bonuses.

## Acquisition and crafting

Eligible for the uncommon item reward pool subject to class restrictions. No bespoke shop price or drop probability is assigned. Craft: Grave Bandage + Feather Keeper → Waxed Fletching. Outgoing recipes: Glass Reed Bow, Winterflight Rack, Burrow Falcon. Each recipe consumes all listed quantities and produces one result when combat starts, requiring one unlocked orthogonally connected packed group. No catalyst or gold cost. Incomplete or disconnected groups consume nothing; an output with no fitting space enters the stash. Larger complete recipes take priority; outputs do not chain at the same combat start.
