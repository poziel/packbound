# Riveted Guard

Common shield. Stable ID: `riveted_guard`.

Timber and iron still stand between a traveler and the dark.

## Purpose and rules

Starting stats: 4 block. Gain its block once at fight start. Block is spent when absorbing damage; it does not regenerate. Thorns, when present, retaliates for every enemy strike.

Duplicate inventory copies add independent contributions. Relics are unique per run. Fight-start block resets with the next fight; round resources refresh each round. Healing is capped at maximum health. No additional duration, cooldown, random proc, or hidden stacking bonus is supplied. No direct empowerment of neighboring items is granted. Normal existing booster effects may still affect eligible attacks and shields.

Provides compact fight-opening protection. Plating preserves block against repeated strikes; healing supports longer fights.

## Visual design

A rounded small plank shield with a thick iron rim, quiet brown boards and a plain round iron boss. Two rivets hold a single central strap.

Footprint is one 32 × 32 px cell; art and mask rotate together. Use deliberate stepped silhouettes and broad material planes. No scattered glitter, colored reflection accents or glow. Semantic layers separate structure, material planes and fittings.
