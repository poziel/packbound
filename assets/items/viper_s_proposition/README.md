# Viper's Proposition

Rare spell; stable ID `viper_s_proposition`. Restricted to mage.

## Purpose and operation

Costs 3 mana per activation. Deals 7 magical damage per affordable activation. Applies 6 poison through the existing combat keyword rules per affordable activation.

Activates in the shared priority and backpack reading order, at most once per ordinary round before explicit repeat effects. All listed activation costs are checked together. No item-specific cooldown, random chance or once-per-fight cap is added. Unaffordable items skip without spending resources. Permanent stats apply only while equipped; combat state resets according to the shared combat rules. Companions are auto-activating equipment, not separately targetable units.

## Strengths and combinations

Compact attacks leave room for support equipment; their resource cost competes with other attacks. Its venom trace participates in the Elementalist's existing reaction system; arrange it with spells of another element. No extra reaction beyond the shared rules is added.

Supplies no adjacency or quiver bonus.

## Acquisition and crafting

Eligible for the rare item reward pool subject to class restrictions. No bespoke shop price or drop probability is assigned. Craft: Antidote Flask + Foxglove Formula + Thorn Whetstone → Viper's Proposition. Outgoing recipes: Orchid Eclipse. Each recipe consumes all listed quantities and produces one result when combat starts, requiring one unlocked orthogonally connected packed group. No catalyst or gold cost. Incomplete or disconnected groups consume nothing; an output with no fitting space enters the stash. Larger complete recipes take priority; outputs do not chain at the same combat start.
