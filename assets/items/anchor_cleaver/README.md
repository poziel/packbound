# Anchor Cleaver

Rare weapon; stable ID `anchor_cleaver`. Restricted to warrior.

## Purpose and operation

Costs 2 stamina per activation. Deals 12 physical damage per affordable activation. Warrior only: requires and spends 2 Momentum; adds 3 damage for each Momentum spent. No cost is spent if activation is unaffordable.

Activates in the shared priority and backpack reading order, at most once per ordinary round before explicit repeat effects. All listed activation costs are checked together. No item-specific cooldown, random chance or once-per-fight cap is added. Unaffordable items skip without spending resources. Permanent stats apply only while equipped; combat state resets according to the shared combat rules. Companions are auto-activating equipment, not separately targetable units.

## Strengths and combinations

A large finishing hit trades early reliability for stored Momentum; a producer or completed exchanges must fund it. Orthogonal adjacency support and available mana/stamina determine the best arrangement. Strategic combinations grant no unlisted bonus.

Supplies no adjacency or quiver bonus.

## Acquisition and crafting

Eligible for the rare item reward pool subject to class restrictions. No bespoke shop price or drop probability is assigned. Craft: Hooked Falchion + Iron Charm → Anchor Cleaver. Outgoing recipes: Mountain's Verdict, Meteor Maul. Each recipe consumes all listed quantities and produces one result when combat starts, requiring one unlocked orthogonally connected packed group. No catalyst or gold cost. Incomplete or disconnected groups consume nothing; an output with no fitting space enters the stash. Larger complete recipes take priority; outputs do not chain at the same combat start.

## Lore

Salvaged from a warship that never reached the sea.
