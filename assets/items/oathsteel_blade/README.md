# Oathsteel Blade

Rare weapon. Stable ID: `oathsteel_blade`.

Sworn names are pressed into the steel while it cools.

## Purpose and rules

Starting stats: 11 damage, 2 pierce. Each affordable normal melee activation costs 2 stamina; damage targets the current enemy. Piercing bypasses that amount of enemy block. Ordinary attacks activate once per round; explicit extra-activation effects may repeat them.

Duplicate inventory copies add independent contributions. Relics are unique per run. Fight-start block resets with the next fight; round resources refresh each round. Healing is capped at maximum health. No additional duration, cooldown, random proc, or hidden stacking bonus is supplied. No direct empowerment of neighboring items is granted. Normal existing booster effects may still affect eligible attacks and shields.

Trades two backpack cells and stamina for a stronger repeatable physical attack. Stamina sources support its activation; damage boosters improve eligible attacks.

An oathbound steel sword with a flared base, blue leather grip and bronze wing-shaped guard. A single broad engraved groove anchors its identity.

Footprint is two vertical 32 px cells, 32 × 64 px total. Art and mask rotate together. Use deliberate stepped silhouettes and broad material planes. No scattered glitter, colored reflection accents or glow. Semantic layers separate structure, material planes and fittings.
