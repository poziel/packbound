# Winter Orrery

Epic spell; stable ID `winter_orrery`. Restricted to mage.

## Purpose and operation

Costs 4 mana per activation. Deals 13 magical damage per affordable activation. Reduces each enemy strike this round by 5.

Activates in the shared priority and backpack reading order, at most once per ordinary round before explicit repeat effects. All listed activation costs are checked together. No item-specific cooldown, random chance or once-per-fight cap is added. Unaffordable items skip without spending resources. Permanent stats apply only while equipped; combat state resets according to the shared combat rules. Companions are auto-activating equipment, not separately targetable units.

## Strengths and combinations

Compact attacks leave room for support equipment; their resource cost competes with other attacks. Its frost trace participates in the Elementalist's existing reaction system; arrange it with spells of another element. No extra reaction beyond the shared rules is added.

Supplies no adjacency or quiver bonus.

## Acquisition and crafting

Eligible for the epic item reward pool subject to class restrictions. No bespoke shop price or drop probability is assigned. Craft: Bottle of Yesterday + Glacier Diagram + Icicle Rosary → Winter Orrery. Outgoing recipes: The Unwritten Sun, Cathedral of Sparks. Each recipe consumes all listed quantities and produces one result when combat starts, requiring one unlocked orthogonally connected packed group. No catalyst or gold cost. Incomplete or disconnected groups consume nothing; an output with no fitting space enters the stash. Larger complete recipes take priority; outputs do not chain at the same combat start.

## Lore

Its moons return to the same night every solstice.
