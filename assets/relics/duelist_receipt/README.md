# Duelist Receipt

Uncommon relic, equipped in a relic slot; no inventory footprint or rotation.

## Purpose and rules

On the opening round, gain +4 physical damage per recipe completed at this fight start, counting at most 3 recipes (+12 maximum). Only completed recipes count: arranging ingredients and viewing a preview grants nothing. Two through five ingredient recipes each count as one completed recipe.

The bonus joins the combined physical attack against the current enemy before resistance and shield absorption. It has no activation cost, cooldown or consumable charges. One copy may be equipped. It adds to other relic awards without repeating item activations. It expires after the opening round; the next fight clears all previous crafting credit. Repeated previews and saving/resuming do not consume credit or multiply the award.

## Strengths and tradeoffs

A freshly assembled loadout earns an opening strike. Saving ingredients for another fight delays their upgraded output and keeps this relic dormant now; carrying completed equipment into the next fight earns no credit. A larger recipe does not earn more credit than a two-item recipe. No hidden adjacency bonus or additional empowerment applies.

## Acquisition and crafting

Available through the uncommon relic reward pool and relic chambers. No purchase price or fixed drop probability is assigned. No recipe produces this relic, and it has no catalysts, outgoing merges or upgrades. Normal inventory recipes require their ingredients to form a connected group; their stated inputs are consumed and each successfully completed recipe earns one credit. Failed recipes consume nothing and earn no credit.

## Lore

The quartermaster signs each assembly receipt in red. The duelists insist the signature is a promise that every newly joined edge will find its mark.

## Visual design

An ivory receipt pierced by a fencing foil seal. The receipt represents the signed assembly record carried into a duel. A centered, readable 32 pixel relic icon uses broad material shading and connected clusters; no colored edge lighting. Art remains unreviewed until explicit visual acceptance.
