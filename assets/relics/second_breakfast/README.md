# Second Breakfast

Common relic, equipped in a relic slot; no inventory footprint or rotation.

## Purpose and rules

On the first round of each fight: gain +3 healing. Conditions use the round before relic awards: one relic cannot enable another in the same round.

This passive has no activation cost, cooldown or consumable charges. Its award is additive with other relics; one copy may be equipped. It targets the player unless awarding damage or enemy status. Direct damage joins the combined attack before resistance and shield absorption; status uses normal cleansing, decay and duration rules. Healing is capped by maximum HP. Each round recomputes the award without retaining counters; fight start resets round conditions. Resource bonuses do not refund already paid costs.

## Strengths and tradeoffs

Build around the stated condition and its scaling limit. This relic supplies no hidden adjacency bonus and does not repeat item activations. Other builds may receive no benefit. Partners satisfying the condition are strategic synergies, with no additional implicit bonus.

## Acquisition and crafting

Available through the common relic reward pool and relic chambers. No purchase price or fixed drop probability is assigned. No crafting recipes, catalysts, outgoing merges or upgrades are defined; no ingredients are consumed.

## Lore

The expedition cook packed an extra meal for the person who would insist they were not hungry. No one remembers who ate first.

## Visual design

Two stacked seed cakes tied in a linen scrap. A centered, readable 32 pixel relic icon using broad material shading and connected clusters; no colored edge lighting. Art remains unreviewed until explicit visual acceptance.
