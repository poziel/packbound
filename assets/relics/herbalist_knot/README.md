# Herbalist Knot

Uncommon relic. Stable ID: `herbalist_knot`.

A healer tied this knot before walking into the marsh.

## Purpose and rules

Starting stats: 1 healing. While owned, add its listed passive stats. Mana and stamina increase their per-round resource capacity; healing restores health each round, plating reduces each enemy strike, and ward refreshes each round against indirect damage. Only the listed stats are granted.

Duplicate inventory copies add independent contributions. Relics are unique per run. Fight-start block resets with the next fight; round resources refresh each round. Healing is capped at maximum health. No additional duration, cooldown, random proc, or hidden stacking bonus is supplied. No direct empowerment of neighboring items is granted. Normal existing booster effects may still affect eligible attacks and shields.

Adds reliable passive support without a backpack footprint. Its rarity is available for future reward filtering; it grants no extra adjacency rules.

## Acquisition and crafting

Joins the ordinary relic offers; relic rarity does not currently gate availability. No shop price or fixed drop probability is defined.

No recipes or outgoing merges.

## Visual design

Two broad green leaves tied around a folded brown linen pouch with an ivory cord knot. Keep the leaf silhouettes separate and the knot thick.

A 32 × 32 px relic illustration for the actual relic/reward slot, without a backpack mask. Use deliberate stepped silhouettes and broad material planes. No scattered glitter, colored reflection accents or glow. Semantic layers separate structure, material planes and fittings.
