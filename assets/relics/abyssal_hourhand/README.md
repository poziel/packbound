# Abyssal Hourhand

Rare relic, equipped in a relic slot; no inventory footprint or rotation.

## Purpose and rules

When the enemy already has Doom before activations: gain +3 magical damage per doom (up to 3 counted). Conditions use the round before relic awards: one relic cannot enable another in the same round.

This passive has no activation cost, cooldown or consumable charges. Its award is additive with other relics; one copy may be equipped. It targets the player unless awarding damage or enemy status. Direct damage joins the combined attack before resistance and shield absorption; status uses normal cleansing, decay and duration rules. Healing is capped by maximum HP. Each round recomputes the award without retaining counters; fight start resets round conditions. Resource bonuses do not refund already paid costs.

## Strengths and tradeoffs

Build around the stated condition and its scaling limit. This relic supplies no hidden adjacency bonus and does not repeat item activations. Other builds may receive no benefit. Partners satisfying the condition are strategic synergies, with no additional implicit bonus.

## Acquisition and crafting

Available through the rare relic reward pool and relic chambers. No purchase price or fixed drop probability is assigned. No crafting recipes, catalysts, outgoing merges or upgrades are defined; no ingredients are consumed.

## Lore

Salvaged from a clock with no face, this hand points toward the hour its owner would rather forget.

## Visual design

A violet clock hand emerging from a black socket. A centered, readable 32 pixel relic icon using broad material shading and connected clusters; no colored edge lighting. Art remains unreviewed until explicit visual acceptance.
