# Soot Jelly

Reflector · Jelly · Floor 8

A floating colony born inside a neglected chimney. Its soot-black bell traps pale embers and lashes out when disturbed.

## Identity and age

7 weeks. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Suspended bell with a rolled rim and five uneven hanging tendrils. Use a violet material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 47 health. Base physical strike damage: 7. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Reflects 2 magical damage once when the combined direct attack damages it. Ward can absorb this.

## Tactics

Ward limits reflected damage; avoid many weak direct attacks.
