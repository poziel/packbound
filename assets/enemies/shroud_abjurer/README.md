# Shroud Abjurer

Purifier · Humanoid · Floor 16

An exiled ward keeper who treats every delver as a contaminant. Her stitched veil hides her face while a forked staff grounds old curses.

## Identity and age

57 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Veiled narrow head, layered violet robes and a forked staff gripped across the body. Use a violet material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 97 health. Base physical strike damage: 11. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Clears existing conditions and elemental traces at the start of every 3th round, before fresh applications.
- Reduces incoming magical damage by 15 percent using the shared resistance rules.

## Tactics

Existing conditions clear every third round; apply fresh effects afterward.
