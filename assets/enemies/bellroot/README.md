# Bellroot

Cycle Guard · Plant · Floor 15

A root colony that has grown through a fallen bronze bell. It closes the bell around its core, then opens to strike with knotted limbs.

## Identity and age

73 growing seasons. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Open bell-shaped flower, dangling clapper seed and wide intertwined root feet. Use a moss material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 96 health. Base physical strike damage: 6 → 15. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Base strike damage cycles through 6 → 15, starting at the first value and repeating.
- Restores 6 temporary block for odd rounds; unused block expires before even rounds.

## Tactics

Odd rounds carry temporary block; even rounds bring the stronger attack.
