# Copper Scarab

Guard · Beetle · Floor 3

A tunnel beetle that polishes discarded coins into its shell. Its territorial scraping sounds like a distant purse being emptied.

## Identity and age

2 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Domed copper shell, paired mandibles and a raised central seam. Use a copper material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 32 health. Base physical strike damage: 5. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Starts combat with 5 block. Absorbed damage spends it permanently for this fight.

## Tactics

Break the finite shell guard; it never regenerates.
