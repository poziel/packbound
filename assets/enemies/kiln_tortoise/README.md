# Kiln Tortoise

Cycle Guard · Tortoise · Floor 17

A furnace beast used to carry hot bricks through narrow tunnels. It withdraws under a kiln-tile shell, then surges forward with its beak.

## Identity and age

94 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Broad tiled dome, low armored head and four thick splayed feet. Use a copper material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 109 health. Base physical strike damage: 5 → 16. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Base strike damage cycles through 5 → 16, starting at the first value and repeating.
- Restores 8 temporary block for odd rounds; unused block expires before even rounds.

## Tactics

Expect temporary block on odd rounds and a heavy even-round strike.
