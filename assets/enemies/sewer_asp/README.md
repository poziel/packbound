# Sewer Asp

Venom · Snake · Floor 5

This patient drain hunter braces its lower coils against stone before striking. Its venom leaves a bitter metallic taste.

## Identity and age

4 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Low double coil, narrow forward head and clearly separated ivory fangs. Use a moss material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 42 health. Base physical strike damage: 6. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Applies 1 poison once per response only if a strike damages player health.

## Tactics

Prevent health damage to stop poison application; carry cleanse.
