# Marrow Heron

Piercer · Heron · Floor 10

A skeletal marsh bird that nests in the flooded ossuary. Its long bill finds gaps between shields as neatly as it once speared fish.

## Identity and age

31 years since binding. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Tall S-shaped neck, long dagger bill, folded wing bones and thin shins. Use a ivory material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 65 health. Base physical strike damage: 9. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Up to 3 damage per strike bypasses block after slow, plating and resistance reduce the strike.

## Tactics

Slow and plating reduce the whole strike before block piercing.
