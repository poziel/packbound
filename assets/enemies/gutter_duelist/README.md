# Gutter Duelist

Parry · Humanoid · Floor 9

A former fencing tutor who sells passage through the drains. He hides a precise blade beneath a ragged red coat and never wastes a flourish.

## Identity and age

29 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Lean masked swordsman, diagonal coat hem and a narrow blade held at the hip. Use a wine material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 57 health. Base physical strike damage: 8. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Restores 5 temporary block every third round. Any remainder expires afterward.

## Tactics

Attack outside every third-round parry window when practical.
