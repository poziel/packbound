# Pallbearer

Opening Guard · Humanoid · Floor 12

The last attendant of a collapsed funeral house still carries an empty coffin lid. It braces the lid at first, then casts it aside.

## Identity and age

63 years at death; 108 years bound. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Broad hooded bearer, coffin-shaped shield and heavy leather boots. Use a umber material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 77 health. Base physical strike damage: 10. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Starts with 8 opening block. Any remainder expires after round two.

## Tactics

Its opening guard expires after round two; avoid wasting burst damage.
