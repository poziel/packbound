# Coffin Mimic

Multi-Strike · Mimic · Floor 16

A corridor predator disguised as a child-sized coffin. It opens sideways and uses the lid as a second jaw.

## Identity and age

34 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Tilted open coffin, thick tooth rows, a tongue and two clawed supporting feet. Use a umber material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 101 health. Base physical strike damage: 6. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Makes 2 strikes per response. Plating and retaliation apply to each strike.
- Restores up to 2 health once after a response that damages player health, if still alive.

## Tactics

Plating handles both jaws; avoid health damage to deny life drain.
