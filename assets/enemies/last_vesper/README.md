# Last Vesper

Purifier · Wraith · Floor 19

The final note of a funeral hymn given a body. It drifts through the last antechamber, unraveling curses that interrupt its endless song.

## Identity and age

Age unknown; first recorded 241 years ago. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Tall floating torn mantle, hollow oval face and a ribbed ivory throat. Use a violet material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 121 health. Base physical strike damage: 11. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Clears existing conditions and elemental traces at the start of every 3th round, before fresh applications.
- Reflects 2 magical damage once when the combined direct attack damages it. Ward can absorb this.

## Tactics

Use ward for reflection and time conditions around third-round purification.
