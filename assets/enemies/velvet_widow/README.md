# Velvet Widow

Venom · Spider · Floor 11

A patient broodmother with red velvet joints. It uses two short bites to hold prey while its young drag the web closed.

## Identity and age

5 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

High pear-shaped abdomen, red joint plates and broad grasping forelegs. Use a wine material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 70 health. Base physical strike damage: 5. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Makes 2 strikes per response. Plating and retaliation apply to each strike.
- Applies 2 poison once per response only if a strike damages player health.

## Tactics

Plating handles both bites; full block also prevents poison.
