# Cellar Tick

Guard · Tick · Floor 2

A swollen scavenger that follows the warmth of occupied bedrolls. It wedges itself into mortar cracks until a traveler passes.

## Identity and age

8 months. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Low oval abdomen, six hooked legs and a pale split headplate. Use a umber material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 26 health. Base physical strike damage: 4. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Starts combat with 4 block. Absorbed damage spends it permanently for this fight.

## Tactics

Spend its small starting block before committing a heavy attack.
