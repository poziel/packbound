# Bramble Crab

Piercer · Crab · Floor 4

Roots growing through its abandoned pot-shell have hardened into thorns. It guards flooded stairs with a single long pincer.

## Identity and age

6 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Asymmetric thorn claw, pot-like shell and four visible bent walking legs. Use a moss material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 38 health. Base physical strike damage: 6. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Up to 1 damage per strike bypasses block after slow, plating and resistance reduce the strike.

## Tactics

Plating and slow reduce the strike before its piercing portion.
