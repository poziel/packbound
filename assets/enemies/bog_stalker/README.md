# Bog Stalker

Venom · Lizard · Floor 8

A long-bodied ambush reptile that uses drowned roots as cover. It raises its throat crest only after a victim steps into reach.

## Identity and age

12 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Four crouched limbs, ridged back, broad throat crest and curling tail. Use a moss material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 51 health. Base physical strike damage: 7. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Applies 2 poison once per response only if a strike damages player health.

## Tactics

Block every point of health damage to prevent its poison.
