# Ossuary Hound

Charger · Hound · Floor 7

A graveyard watchdog assembled from several loyal animals. It lowers its skull in a silent warning before lunging across the corridor.

## Identity and age

17 years since binding. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Deep ribcage, canine skull, weight-bearing paws and a lifted segmented tail. Use a ivory material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 54 health. Base physical strike damage: 0 → 14. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

## Tactics

Use its harmless warning round to prepare for the heavy lunge.
