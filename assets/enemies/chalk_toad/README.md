# Chalk Toad

Sustain · Toad · Floor 6

Water dripping through old limestone has turned this toad almost white. It squats beside mineral pools and swallows loose torch embers.

## Identity and age

9 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Wide flat jaw, bulging eyes, splayed forefeet and two folded haunches. Use a ivory material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 46 health. Base physical strike damage: 6. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Restores up to 3 health after its response if still alive, capped at maximum health.

## Tactics

Push damage above its healing rate; poison and burn provide pressure.
