# Reliquary Fungus

Sustain · Fungus · Floor 11

A walking shelf fungus that has grown around a forgotten saint box. The box is empty, but the creature still protects it.

## Identity and age

46 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Tiered asymmetrical caps, thick root feet and a small ivory chest inset. Use a moss material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 74 health. Base physical strike damage: 8. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Restores up to 4 health after its response if still alive, capped at maximum health.

## Tactics

Sustain pressure through its healing; use stronger damage over time.
