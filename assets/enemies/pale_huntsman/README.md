# Pale Huntsman

Piercer · Humanoid · Floor 17

A spectral gamekeeper who still follows trespassers through the lower halls. His spear is solid bone, even when his cloak seems weightless.

## Identity and age

38 years at death; 91 years bound. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Antlered closed mask, split pale cloak and long upright bone spear. Use a ivory material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 105 health. Base physical strike damage: 12. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Up to 4 damage per strike bypasses block after slow, plating and resistance reduce the strike.
- Starts with 4 opening block. Any remainder expires after round two.

## Tactics

Slow and plating soften piercing; opening guard expires after two rounds.
