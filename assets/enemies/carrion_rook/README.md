# Carrion Rook

Sunder · Bird · Floor 5

A clever crypt bird that pries buckles apart before pecking exposed skin. It keeps stolen rings tucked beneath one wing.

## Identity and age

11 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Angular hooked bill, hunched feather mantle and long gripping talons. Use a slate material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 40 health. Base physical strike damage: 6. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Destroys up to 2 player block before striking.

## Tactics

Its opening sunder removes a little block; plating remains reliable.
