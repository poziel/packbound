# Pitch Eel

Drainer · Eel · Floor 14

An oil-black eel that travels between pools by dragging its muscular body. It feeds on warmth and closes torn skin after a successful bite.

## Identity and age

16 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Long hooked jaw, continuous dorsal fin and a tight upright tail loop. Use a wine material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 85 health. Base physical strike damage: 10. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Restores up to 4 health once after a response that damages player health, if still alive.

## Tactics

Prevent all health damage to deny its healing trigger.
