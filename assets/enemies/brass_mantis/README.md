# Brass Mantis

Multi-Strike · Mantis · Floor 12

A surgical automaton left to maintain the crypt machinery. Its three measured cuts follow the same inspection routine every round.

## Identity and age

37 years since assembly. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Tall narrow thorax, triangular head and two large folded scythe arms. Use a copper material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 69 health. Base physical strike damage: 4. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Makes 3 strikes per response. Plating and retaliation apply to each strike.

## Tactics

Plating and thorns each benefit from its three separate strikes.
