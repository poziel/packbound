# Tallow Slug

Sustain · Slug · Floor 3

Once bred to clean lamp trays, these slugs now feed on the fatty runoff of the crypt kitchens. Their wounds close with pale wax.

## Identity and age

14 months. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Heavy tapering foot, upright eyestalks and overlapping wax folds. Use a ivory material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 35 health. Base physical strike damage: 4. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Restores up to 2 health after its response if still alive, capped at maximum health.

## Tactics

Sustained damage must exceed its modest regeneration.
