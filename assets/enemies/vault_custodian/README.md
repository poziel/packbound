# Vault Custodian

Sunder · Golem · Floor 19

The lower vault still recognizes this brass construct as its only keyholder. It turns its entire torso to drive a heavy lock-shaped fist forward.

## Identity and age

312 years since assembly. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Massive lock torso, separate piston legs and a round recessed keyhole face. Use a copper material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 126 health. Base physical strike damage: 11 → 14. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Base strike damage cycles through 11 → 14, starting at the first value and repeating.
- Destroys up to 3 player block before striking.
- Starts combat with 6 block. Absorbed damage spends it permanently for this fight.

## Tactics

Plating survives sunder; its alternating heavy attack rewards strong defenses.
