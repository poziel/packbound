# Salt Mirror

Reflector · Crystal · Floor 13

A mineral growth that learned to imitate the figures reflected in its faces. It hovers on a narrow point above the salt floor.

## Identity and age

112 years since formation. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Uneven fan of thick crystal blades surrounding a dark central face. Use a violet material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 79 health. Base physical strike damage: 9. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Reflects 3 magical damage once when the combined direct attack damages it. Ward can absorb this.
- Reduces incoming magical damage by 15 percent using the shared resistance rules.

## Tactics

Ward protects from reflection; physical damage avoids its magic resistance.
