# Needle Spider

Multi-Strike · Spider · Floor 6

Its front legs have evolved into stitching needles. It tests a traveler with two precise taps before returning to its web.

## Identity and age

2 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Eight articulated legs, compact striped abdomen and long needle forelegs. Use a umber material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 43 health. Base physical strike damage: 3. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Makes 2 strikes per response. Plating and retaliation apply to each strike.
- Up to 1 damage per strike bypasses block after slow, plating and resistance reduce the strike.

## Tactics

Plating reduces both strikes, including their piercing damage.
