# Blood Orchid

Drainer · Plant · Floor 18

A carnivorous bloom cultivated in the old infirmary. It turns toward a wounded traveler with the patient attention of a nurse.

## Identity and age

19 growing seasons. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Five broad red petals, ivory inner teeth and a bent stem on gripping roots. Use a wine material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 116 health. Base physical strike damage: 11. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Restores up to 5 health once after a response that damages player health, if still alive.
- Restores up to 2 health after its response if still alive, capped at maximum health.

## Tactics

Full block denies drain; sustained damage must also beat regeneration.
