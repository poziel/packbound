# Crypt Fencer

Parry · Humanoid · Floor 13

A ceremonial guard who remembers only the final lesson of her fencing school. She answers every third exchange with a practiced guard.

## Identity and age

41 years at death; 52 years bound. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Closed swept helmet, short steel cape and a forward-pointed rapier. Use a slate material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 83 health. Base physical strike damage: 10. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Restores 7 temporary block every third round. Any remainder expires afterward.
- Up to 1 damage per strike bypasses block after slow, plating and resistance reduce the strike.

## Tactics

Watch the third-round block and keep enough plating for piercing.
