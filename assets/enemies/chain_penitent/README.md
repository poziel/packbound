# Chain Penitent

Enrager · Humanoid · Floor 18

An armored prisoner who counts every blow as another broken vow. Two short chain lashes become harder to withstand the longer the fight lasts.

## Identity and age

46 years at death; 137 years bound. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Bent closed helmet, iron shoulder yoke and two visible hanging chain loops. Use a slate material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 113 health. Base physical strike damage: 7. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Makes 2 strikes per response. Plating and retaliation apply to each strike.
- Gains 1 attack damage for each elapsed round; resets next combat.

## Tactics

Use plating against both strikes and finish before fury grows.
