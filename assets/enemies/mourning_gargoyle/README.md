# Mourning Gargoyle

Guard · Gargoyle · Floor 15

A roof guardian brought below ground after its chapel fell. It crouches over an imaginary roof edge and bares its stone teeth at passing lights.

## Identity and age

206 years since carving. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Crouching stone torso, folded angular wings, thick claws and two short horns. Use a slate material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 99 health. Base physical strike damage: 10. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Starts combat with 6 block. Absorbed damage spends it permanently for this fight.
- Reduces incoming physical damage by 15 percent using the shared resistance rules.

## Tactics

Magic avoids its physical resistance; its starting block remains finite.
