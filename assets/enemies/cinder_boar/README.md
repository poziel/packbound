# Cinder Boar

Enrager · Boar · Floor 10

A furnace-yard boar whose bristles have fused into charcoal ridges. Every exchange makes its stubborn forward charge more dangerous.

## Identity and age

8 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Heavy low shoulders, split tusks, bristled spine and short braced legs. Use a copper material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 69 health. Base physical strike damage: 8. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Gains 1 attack damage for each elapsed round; resets next combat.

## Tactics

End the fight promptly before its attack growth overwhelms defenses.
