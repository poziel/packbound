# Shroud Moth

Reflector · Moth · Floor 4

A funerary moth that mistakes breathing travelers for warm burial lamps. The markings on its wings return a little of every direct blow.

## Identity and age

19 days. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Two broad scalloped wings, eye-like cream markings and a narrow furry abdomen. Use a violet material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 33 health. Base physical strike damage: 5. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Reflects 1 magical damage once when the combined direct attack damages it. Ward can absorb this.

## Tactics

Ward absorbs reflection; damage over time avoids direct-hit reflection.
