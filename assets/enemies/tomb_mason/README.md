# Tomb Mason

Sunder · Golem · Floor 9

This unfinished stone worker still tries to repair the dungeon around living intruders. Its chisel-like fist knocks defensive stances apart.

## Identity and age

84 years since carving. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Square stone shoulders, separated block fingers and a recessed amber face. Use a slate material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 61 health. Base physical strike damage: 8. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Starts combat with 3 block. Absorbed damage spends it permanently for this fight.
- Destroys up to 2 player block before striking.

## Tactics

Plating withstands its sunder; the initial guard does not refill.
