# Chain Snail

Guard · Snail · Floor 7

A slow scavenger that grows a spiral of iron links around its soft body. It has crossed the same stairwell for longer than most guards remember.

## Identity and age

23 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Large spiral shell, low muscular foot and a pair of blunt feelers. Use a slate material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 58 health. Base physical strike damage: 6. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Starts combat with 7 block. Absorbed damage spends it permanently for this fight.

## Tactics

Its guard is finite; damage over time can wear it down.
