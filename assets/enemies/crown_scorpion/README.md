# Crown Scorpion

Venom · Scorpion · Floor 14

A crypt scorpion that wears a ring of grave gold across its head. Its heavy pincers hold a target steady for the crowned tail.

## Identity and age

13 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Twin broad claws, six low walking legs and a high segmented stinger arch. Use a copper material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 87 health. Base physical strike damage: 9. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Starts combat with 4 block. Absorbed damage spends it permanently for this fight.
- Applies 3 poison once per response only if a strike damages player health.

## Tactics

Break its initial block and keep cleanse available for poison.
