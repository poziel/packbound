# Wick Bat

Skirmisher · Bat · Floor 2

This cave bat collects candle wax on its ears. It hunts by the sound of buckles, swooping twice before retreating to the rafters.

## Identity and age

3 years. This is a typical encountered individual, not a universal species lifespan. Its behavior is territorial and bound to the dungeon; it does not speak or bargain during combat.

## Appearance

Broad folded membrane wings, pointed ears and a short hanging body. Use a wine material palette with broad shaded planes and readable separated limbs. Face the delver with a slight three-quarter turn; preserve the complete anatomy and convincing weight or airborne suspension. Avoid decorative rim lighting and scattered texture.

## Combat

Starts with 24 health. Base physical strike damage: 2. All abilities reset at the beginning of each fight; illustrated equipment grants only the protection stated below.

- Makes 2 strikes per response. Plating and retaliation apply to each strike.

## Tactics

Plating reduces both bites; thorns punish repeated contact.
